The brief

A coastal retreat that needed to book as well as it photographs

No. 1 Longshore is a luxury let at Porth, Cornwall — sleeping eight, thirty seconds from the cove, with hotel-level finishes. The site had to sell the stay in a glance, make Book Direct obvious, and still leave a clear path for guests who prefer Airbnb.

  • check_circle Lead with photography and a one-line promise: the perfect coastal retreat.
  • check_circle Put Book Direct and Book via Airbnb side by side so commission is a choice, not the default.
  • check_circle Show live availability so enquiries do not bounce to a third-party calendar.
  • check_circle Surface facilities, guest reviews and WhatsApp so trust is built before anyone messages the host.

This project

The No. 1 Longshore website — answered

Book Direct, availability and a guest book for a Porth coastal let.

What was the brief for No. 1 Longshore?

A photography-led booking site for a Porth coastal retreat: Book Direct beside Airbnb, live availability, and a guest book that lives on the property domain — facilities such as parking, pets, kitchen and workspace shown as facts, not a buried FAQ.

Why put Book Direct next to Airbnb?

Guests already know the listing platforms. Sitting Book Direct beside them makes the cheaper, direct stay obvious without pretending Airbnb does not exist. Availability has to be accurate or the path fails.

Can you build a holiday-let website like this?

Yes. Coastal and rural lets need photography, clear house rules, booking and a reason to go direct. Commerce Enterprise is the usual fit when booking tools are involved; a consultation confirms the stack.

Does the guest book have to be on Airbnb only?

No. For Longshore the guest book sits on the property domain so reviews and stories strengthen the branded site, not only the listing platform.
