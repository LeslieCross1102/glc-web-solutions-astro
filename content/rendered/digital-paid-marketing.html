Google Ads and Meta Ads — answered

Setup from £550, monthly management, landing pages, and how you see what spend produced.

How much does it cost to set up Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Setup and launch is £550 one-off and covers two platforms — Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) — including campaign structure, tracking, audience and keyword research, launch creative, conversion foundations and handover.

What does the £150 per month Maintain package include?

Ongoing management so spend stays efficient: bi-weekly insights and consultation, budget management, insight reports from the data, and creative refreshes. Commit annually and the £550 setup fee is waived.

When would I choose the £500 per month full-service package?

When you need new landing pages and ongoing page optimisation as well as media buying. It includes everything in Maintain, plus lookalike and retargeting strategy and reporting across platforms.

Is ad spend included in those fees?

No. The packages are for setup and management. Media spend sits in your Google and Meta accounts so you can see exactly what went out and what came back.

Will I know what the ads actually produced?

Yes. Conversion tracking is in place before launch, then reporting and attribution connect spend to enquiries, bookings or sales — not vanity reach. That is the point of the process: audit, structure, launch, then optimise with numbers you can act on.

Do I need a website before running ads?

You need a page that can convert. If the current site leaks traffic, we fix landing pages as part of the full-service package — or we start with the website. Paid media on a weak page burns budget.
