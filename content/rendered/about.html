Industry insights at the AOP

The Association of Online Publishers is the UK industry body for digital publishers — newsbrands, magazines, broadcasters and digital-only titles. Formed in 2002, it exists to champion original content and keep publishers in the same conversations as each other and the platforms.

AOP Crunch is their working series: short, practical sessions where publisher members and industry partners compare notes on live issues — traffic, advertising, AI and audience — rather than sitting through a stage show.
