This project

The My Retreat Garden Rooms website — answered

What did My Retreat Garden Rooms need online?

A premium showcase for Surrey homeowners: imagery-led design, local SEO foundations, and a clear path to request a quote for a garden room or studio.

How does the site help people request a quote?

The layout leads with the product, then process and contact — so someone who has been browsing studios can enquire without hunting. Quote journeys sit with the photography, not in a footer afterthought.

Is this useful for other home-improvement showcases?

Yes. Garden rooms, extensions, joinery and similar visual products need the work on screen and a low-friction quote. Bliss Stairs is a related example if the product is more crafted-to-order than a standard range.

Does local SEO matter if most leads come from referrals?

Referrals still check you online. Local SEO foundations mean the site supports those searches and captures people in Surrey who found you without a personal introduction.
