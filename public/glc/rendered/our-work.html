The through-line is local businesses that need the site to produce enquiries, not look pretty in a portfolio.

Can I see live examples, not just screenshots?

Yes. Each case study includes device mockups and a path through to the live site where it is public. Our Work is the index; open a study for the brief, deliverables and results.

Do you only work with trades?

Trades are a strong fit — electricians, plumbers, landscapers — but so are pubs, holiday lets, coaches and other sole traders. If the job is a clear website that saves you time, it is in scope.

Can you build something similar for my business?

Yes. Case studies are examples, not templates you are stuck with. A free consultation maps what your site actually needs — quote flows, bookings, galleries, local search — then a package is chosen to match.
