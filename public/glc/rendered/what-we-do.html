Choosing a service

Where to start, and what sits around the website

Website design, SEO & GEO, paid ads and consultancy — you do not have to buy all four.

What services does GLC Web Solutions offer?

Four connected offerings: website design and development from £445, SEO & GEO audits, Google Ads and Meta Ads from £550 setup, and digital and AI consultancy shaped by 27 years at The Telegraph. Start with what you need now and add the rest without switching agencies.

Do I have to buy website, SEO and ads together?

No. Most clients start with the website. Search, ads and consultancy are added only where they save time and money.

Where should a sole trader start?

With a site that is clear on a phone and easy to enquire through. If people already know you but cannot find you, add SEO & GEO. If you need enquiries faster than search will allow, add paid ads. If you are stuck on a digital decision, book consultancy.

Who are these services for?

Sole traders and small businesses — trades, pubs, holiday lets, coaches and local services across London and the South-East — who want professional work at a cost-effective price, without sales waffle.

How do I see prices for each service?

The Costs page lists every published package in one place. SEO and consultancy stay quoted after a conversation because the work depends on the site in front of us. A free consultation is always available.
