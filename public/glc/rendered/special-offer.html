    Free design, seven-day build — answered

    How the risk-free design works, what £445 includes, and what happens if you are not happy.

    Is the website design really free?

    Yes. You see a professional design first, shaped around a short brief. You are under no obligation to pay anything if you are not happy with it. You only pay if you approve the design and commission the build.

    What happens if I do not like the design?

    You pay nothing. There is no obligation to proceed. If you want tweaks, there is a further day to mark them up before the build starts so the live site matches what you signed off.

    How does the seven-day website work?

    Share a brief plus whatever you already have — logo, photos, the core facts. A design comes back in about 48 hours. After revisions, the finished site is delivered within another 48 hours: listed on Google and Bing, with Google Analytics, professional hosting and daily backups.

    What does the £445 build include?

    £445 is the Professional Basic package if you go ahead: up to 5 pages, mobile-responsive design, contact form, basic on-page SEO, SSL, daily backups, a test server, Search Console, Analytics, one round of revisions, and launch training. Larger builds are Enterprise Plus at £995 and Commerce Enterprise at £3,495.

    Can you redesign my existing site as well as start from scratch?

    Yes. The offer covers a brand-new site from a brief, or a redesign that keeps what already works and elevates the rest — clearer structure, sharper visuals, a modern experience without a blank page.

    Is there a consultation before design work starts?

    Yes. You talk through objectives with Gareth first so the plan matches the business, not a generic template — what success looks like, which pages matter, and a practical path to deliver it.
